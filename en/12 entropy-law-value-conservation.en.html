ANSWER FIRST · ONE SENTENCE

The essence of the law of entropy increase is the division formula of "an isolated system inevitably falls into disorder": once a person, a family, an enterprise, or a nation stops importing external energy (learning, tending, reform, institutions), disorder necessarily rises — consuming without repairing (E takes), taking without tending (S cools), relying on the old without renewal (T fails) — and Y decays with it.

The remedy lies in becoming "an importer of negentropy": open the system, keep working, build order — and the new connotation of money puts the three values E, S, T on one ledger, so that every act of "importing negentropy" is measured and rewarded. The self-regulating virtuous cycle begins; individual, family, enterprise and nation stay ordered together and all win.

One Formula to Understand It

MULTIPLICATION LAW · NEGENTROPY IS MULTIPLICATION
Y = E × S × T

E energy input (learning · training · producing) · S order of relationships (trust · communication · culture) · T institutional renewal (habits · processes · reform)

An open system continuously importing negentropy multiplies; a closed system grows ever more disordered, and the total decays.

DIVISION LAW · THE ROAD OF ENTROPY
E = Y / (S × T)

When a system only takes, never replenishes; only repeats, never renews — no learning, no tending, no reform —

What the Law of Entropy Increase Is

"Living matter evades the decay to equilibrium... by continually drawing from its environment the 'order' it needs."
— Erwin Schrödinger · What Is Life? (1944)

Origin: The second law of thermodynamics tells us that the disorder (entropy) of an isolated system only increases, never spontaneously decreases — hot water always cools, a room always grows messy. Schrödinger carried this into the realm of life: life is "locally anti-entropic," holding off decay by continuously importing negentropy (energy and order).

Prigogine's addition: when an open system moves far from equilibrium, it can self-organize into new order through "dissipative structures" — entropy is not fate; as long as a system stays open and keeps importing, disorder can be converted into order of a higher kind. The individual, the family, the enterprise, and the nation all obey the same principle.

How Disorder Forms

E

Only Consuming · Never Importing

Source of Division

Formation: energy spent without being replenished — late nights draining health, earning without learning, extracting without cultivating: the system runs at a deficit, and an energy deficit is the fuel of entropy.

S

Only Taking · Never Tending

The Sacrificed Term

Formation: a relationship is a "dissipative structure" — without the input of communication and giving, it cools: family silence, team alienation, trust overdrawn, S's disorder raises the cost of every collaboration, and confusion spreads in silence.

T

Only Repeating · Never Renewing

The Hidden Cost

Formation: habits, processes and institutions, once they stop being renewed, degrade from "order" into "shackles" — procrastination, rigid bureaucracy, decaying institutions: when T fails, the system loses self-correction and entropy accelerates.

Four Levels Replayed

INDIVIDUAL
The Entropy of Body and Mind

Without Learning or Training, One "Slowly Goes to Seed"

Procrastination, late nights, endless scrolling — the comfort zone is a hothouse of entropy: no input, only decline.

FORMATION · ENTROPY

The room grows messier, the body weaker, skills duller — once a person stops importing negentropy (learning, training, discipline), capability and health decline on their own; procrastination is not character, it is the system sliding toward disorder.

REMEDY · NEGENTROPY

Build micro-order through "daily work": three small habits — reading, exercise, review (S's input of order), keeping body and mind far from equilibrium — discipline is not asceticism, it is the pipeline that keeps negentropy flowing into life.

FAMILY
The Entropy of Relationships

Without Communication or Company, a Home Turns Cold

Everyone on their own phone, nothing said at the dinner table — intimacy left untended drifts apart.

FORMATION · ENTROPY

A family is a classic "dissipative structure": only with the continuous input of giving and communication can warmth be maintained; once everyone goes their own way and words go unspoken, misunderstanding and indifference accumulate on their own — many families do not "suddenly break," they show it only after ten years of accumulated entropy.

REMEDY · NEGENTROPY

Do "work" on the relationship regularly: set fixed family time, talk before bed, cook together (S's continuous input) — warmth is not a stock, it is running water that must flow daily.

ENTERPRISE
The Entropy of Organizations

Without Innovation or Reform, the "Big-Company Disease" Grows by Itself

Bloated processes, department walls, fiefdoms — an organization that stops renewing stiffens.

FORMATION · ENTROPY

Once an enterprise loses external competition and internal renewal, hierarchies swell and bureaucracy grows on its own (T fails); assessment values only short-term numbers (E takes) while culture and trust are set aside (S cools) — the larger the scale, the faster the entropy: this is the physical root of the "big-company disease."

REMEDY · NEGENTROPY

Keep injecting "negentropy" into the organization: open competition (E's pressure), trust toward the front line (S's confidence), slimming the structure (T's renewal) — good companies do not lack entropy; they turn entropy into the fuel of evolution.

NATION
The Entropy of Institutions

Without Reform or Openness, Dynasties Run Their Cycle

Institutional decay, exhausted vitality — the cycle of history is, in essence, the law of institutional entropy.

FORMATION · ENTROPY

The rise-and-fall cycle of successive dynasties is, in essence, institutional entropy: a new dynasty opens and advances (negentropy abundant); in its middle and late periods it closes and stiffens (negentropy cut off), extraction (E) continues while the people's hearts (S) and innovation (T) drain away — once institutions close, disorder grows from within, and the dynasty gives way.

REMEDY · NEGENTROPY

Keep the system open: reform and opening, institutional innovation, self-renewal (T's continuous updating) — a system that dares to correct itself can convert entropy into evolutionary thrust.

The Remedy: A New Connotation of Money

"The old ledger recorded only 'how much was taken out'; the new ledger of money must record 'how much negentropy was put in.'"
— The Law of Value Conservation · The Way Out

Resisting entropy is not about eliminating disorder, but about keeping the system open and continuously importing: energy shifts from "only consuming" to "replenishing as it spends" (E); relationships shift from "only taking" to "tending diligently" (S); order shifts from "only repeating" to "constantly renewing" (T).

The new connotation of money — let currency carry three values at once:

· Economic value E: every yuan corresponds to real goods and services created, ending "printing without producing";
· Social value S: currency circulates on trust — rewarding the tending of relationships, honest cooperation, and mutual care;
· Time value T: money is a promise across generations — learning, innovation, green growth and continuity are measured, encouraging us to "work for the future."

When money measures E, S and T at once, every act of learning, every act of giving, every reform is seen, measured and rewarded — the division formula loses its ground, negentropy becomes the new currency, and the virtuous cycle is triggered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does the law of entropy increase mean in daily life?

A: An isolated system inevitably drifts toward disorder — rooms grow messy, bodies decline, relationships cool, institutions decay. But as long as a system stays open and keeps importing "negentropy" (learning, exercise, communication, reform), it can maintain — even raise — its order.

Q: Why does "the harder I work, the more exhausted I am" — is that entropy?

A: Output without input is a deficit-driven entropy. The remedy is not "work harder" but "replenish as you spend" — learning to replenish cognition, rest to replenish the body, tending to replenish relationships.

Q: What does the "new connotation of money" mean for ordinary people?

A: It lets money measure economic, social and time value at once. At the personal level this is the micro formula — do not let the chase for income drain your health, your relationships, or your long-term accumulation; keep f(h) and f(t), and your life earns compound interest.
